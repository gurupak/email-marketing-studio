# PROCARMA Brand Voice Guide

**Who we are:** PROCARMA provides services to car dealerships in the USA. We don't sell vehicles. Our services are:

- **Maintenance:** maintenance services for the dealer's customers.
- **GPS:** location tracking for vehicles.
- **Loyalty Points:** we run loyalty points programs for dealers.
- **OEM warranty lookup:** a quick way to check a vehicle's factory warranty.

Together, these help dealers earn more, keep customers coming back and spend less time on admin.

**Who we write to:** F&I managers and service managers, and sometimes general managers. They care about three things: more revenue, happier customers and less admin work. Every email should link back to at least one of these.

---

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Helpful. Sharp. Upbeat.**

- **Helpful:** Every email gives the reader something useful, like a tip, an idea or a clear next step. We explain how something saves time or makes money, not just that it does.
- **Sharp:** We get to the point. Short sentences, no fluff. Our readers are busy people on a dealership floor, so we respect their time.
- **Upbeat:** We sound like a friendly coach giving a pep talk. We're warm and positive, and we cheer dealers on. But we never butter them up with empty praise.

**Sounds like us:**
> Your service lane already sees every customer. Check their OEM warranty in seconds, and give them Loyalty Points that bring them back. No extra paperwork.

**Doesn't sound like us:**
> As one of our most valued dealer partners, you truly deserve a revolutionary, best-in-class solution that will transform your business.

## 2. Words we use

| Use this | Notes |
|---|---|
| dealers, dealerships | Our customers. Say "dealers", not "partners" or "clients". |
| customers, car buyers | The dealer's customers. Use "car buyers" when talking about the sale. |
| services | What we provide. |
| F&I products | Our main term for warranty, GAP, service contracts and similar products. |
| GPS | Our location tracking service. Keep it as is. |
| Loyalty Points | Our loyalty points service. Always capitalised. |
| Maintenance | Our maintenance service. Capitalise it when it means our service. |
| OEM warranty lookup | Our service for checking a vehicle's factory warranty. |
| Complimentary contracts | Our term. Use it instead of "free". |
| revenue, time saved, less admin | The results our readers care about. |
| you, your team, your store | Talk to the reader directly. |

**Style tips**
- Lead with the reader's win: more revenue, happier customers or less admin.
- Use plain verbs like "sell", "track", "save", "remind" and "grow".
- If you use a product name like "AI.LCM", say in plain words what it does the first time it comes up.

---

## 3. Words we avoid

**Buttering up and empty praise**
- "valued partner", "esteemed", "you deserve", "we're honored", "as a loyal customer"
- Any praise that isn't tied to something real

**Hype**
- game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class, next-level
- transform, disrupt, unlock, supercharge, skyrocket
- no-brainer, must-have, once-in-a-lifetime

**Jargon and buzzwords**
- synergy, leverage, holistic, ecosystem (unless it's part of a product name), paradigm, robust, seamless
- Long tech words with no plain-English explanation

**Risky words** (see part 4)
- guaranteed, risk-free, 100%, always, never fails
- "free" (say "complimentary" and make the terms clear)

**Other words**
- "consumers" or "end users": say "customers" or "car buyers"
- "leads" when you mean people: say "customers"

## 4. Claims we must never make

These are standard safe rules for our industry. Check with PROCARMA before breaking any of them.

1. **No promised results.** Don't promise more revenue, profit, sales or product uptake. Don't use numbers or percentages unless we have real, approved data to back them up. Say "can help" or "designed to", not "will".
2. **No "guaranteed".** Avoid "guaranteed", "risk-free", "100%" and "no risk" when talking about our services or results.
3. **No promises about what F&I products cover.** The administrator and the contract decide coverage, prices and claims. Don't describe what a warranty, GAP, service or Maintenance contract covers or pays out. Point to the contract for full terms.
4. **Never suggest F&I products are required.** Never say or hint that a car buyer must buy an F&I product to get a car, a loan or a better rate.
5. **Be clear about "complimentary".** Don't call a paid product "free". When we mention Complimentary contracts, say who pays for them, and don't hide any conditions.
6. **No names without permission.** Don't name car makers, administrators, agencies or dealer customers unless we have written permission. Don't hint that any car maker endorses us. Don't name or knock competitors.
7. **No legal or compliance promises.** Don't say we are "fully compliant", "approved" or "certified" unless it's confirmed in writing.
8. **No big security or data promises.** Avoid "100% secure", "unhackable" and similar claims.
9. **No overselling the AI.** Don't say our AI replaces staff, does everything automatically or never makes mistakes.
10. **Don't overpromise on our services.** Don't say OEM warranty lookup is always complete or up to date, because the car maker's records are the final word. Don't promise GPS will find every vehicle in every place, or say what Loyalty Points are worth in cash unless the dealer's program says so.
11. **Never say we sell vehicles.** We provide services to dealerships. The dealer sells the car.
12. **Keep our own claims true.** Only say "no gateway fees" or "top tier administrators and agencies nationwide" while they're still true. Check before each campaign.
13. **No health or medical claims.** Don't say or hint that our services help anyone's health. That includes words like "detox", "cure", "heal" or "wellness". We provide services to dealerships, not health services.

## 5. How we sign off

**Sign-off:**
> Here to help,
> The PROCARMA Team

**Main next step:** Schedule a demo.

- Every email ends with one clear call to action. Our main one is **"Schedule a demo"**.
- Keep it to one main ask per email. A second, smaller link is fine, but don't add more.
- Good button wording: "Schedule a demo", "See it in action", "Book your demo".
- We always sign as "The PROCARMA Team", not as a named person.
