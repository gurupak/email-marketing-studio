# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/`: the business's voice, tone, colours, logo and key messages.
- `leads/`: information about who we're writing to (audience notes and segments, not real mailing lists).
- `campaigns/`: one subfolder per campaign, holding its plan and the emails for it.
- `notes/`: research, decisions and anything worth remembering.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words, and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Draft and preview only. Tests go to test addresses I give you.
  - Sending through Resend is allowed only to Resend's test addresses (ending in `@resend.dev`) and to my own email address, irfan.pak@gmail.com. Nothing else.
  - A guard will enforce this. It's coming in the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
