Pit Stop Maintenance is our new Maintenance service that links your F&I desk and your service lane. It's designed to keep admin low.

**Launch offer:** Customers who buy it get one complimentary oil change after 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first, and 5% off one tire rotation after 6 months. See the contract for what it covers.

Here's who does what, from the sale to the service visit.

**At the sale: your F&I managers**

They offer it to car buyers as an option, at a price your store sets. It's one more clear option that can help grow revenue.

Tip: Say it's optional up front. Try this: "This one's optional. You don't need it to get your car, your loan or your rate. The contract has the full terms."

**At the visit: your service team**

The service desk applies the launch offer when the customer comes in. That can help bring customers back to your service lane.

Tip: Check the purchase date first. Both parts of the offer are counted from it.

**Behind the scenes: PROCARMA**

We hold a short setup call with your general manager. Then we train your F&I and service teams. After that, we track sales and return visits and run the systems behind the service. Each month, we send you a short summary of how many your team sold and how many customers came back for service.

Tip for general managers: Bring your F&I and service managers to your one video demo, so each team sees its part.
