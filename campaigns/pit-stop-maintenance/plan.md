## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Pit Stop Maintenance launch
- **Summary:** Six emails over four weeks invite about 200 waitlisted dealerships to book a demo and start a subscription, with most of the effort in the first two weeks.
- **Main goal:** 30 of about 200 dealerships (15%) start a subscription between December 7, 2026 and January 3, 2027.
- **Second goals:** Book demos with stores that aren't ready yet, and learn which group responds best.

**Name options**

| Name | Why it could work |
|---|---|
| **Pit Stop Maintenance** (recommended) | It's short and upbeat, and it says what the service is. |
| Service Lane Maintenance | It's the plainest option, and it speaks directly to service teams. |
| Road Ready Maintenance | It's friendly, and it focuses on the customer's car. |
| Comeback Maintenance | It points to customers returning, but it could sound odd to car buyers. |

## 2. Target audience

We group people by job role. We don't yet know how many people are in each group. The group names are for planning only and won't appear in emails.

| Group | Who they are | Main interest | What they may worry about | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Deal Desk** | F&I managers | More revenue | Does this slow down the sale? How do I explain it clearly? | Interested, needs details |
| **The Service Lane** | Service managers | Happier customers who come back | Can my team handle the visits? How does it work at the desk? | Interested, needs details |
| **The Store Leaders** | General managers | Results for the whole store, with less admin | What does it cost? Who runs it? Is it worth the team's time? | Likely makes the final decision |

- **Matching job roles:** Job titles will vary, so sort them into these three groups before launch. Anyone who doesn't fit joins The Store Leaders.
- **Where they are:** They're busy on the dealership floor and read email in short bursts, so emails must be short and easy to read on a phone.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Pit Stop Maintenance is a maintenance service for your customers, designed to give them a reason to come back to your service lane without adding much admin for your team.

| Group | Supporting message | What we can show |
|---|---|---|
| The Deal Desk | One more clear option to offer car buyers, which can help grow revenue. | A short example of how to explain it. It must be clear that buying it is optional. |
| The Service Lane | Can help bring customers back to your service lane. | The steps from sale to service visit. |
| The Store Leaders | One service that can help with revenue, return visits and admin. | The price, the setup steps and who does what. We need the real steps before we can claim less admin. |
| Everyone | See it first, then decide. A demo shows you how it works. | A demo and clear terms. |

**Proof:** We have no approved results yet, so we won't use numbers, results or dealer names. Emails explain how the service works instead.

**Brand rules to check on every email**

- Say "can help" or "designed to", never "will" (rule 1).
- Don't use "guaranteed" or "risk-free" (rule 2).
- Don't describe what a contract covers, and point to the contract instead (rule 3).
- Never suggest a car buyer must buy this to get a car, a loan or a better rate (rule 4).
- Say "complimentary", never "free", and say who pays (rule 5).
- Don't name car makers, administrators, dealers or competitors without written permission (rule 6).
- Use "no gateway fees" or "top tier administrators and agencies nationwide" only if you've checked they're still true (rule 12).
- Keep the 30 target and all other goals in this plan out of the emails (rule 1).
- Sign off with "Here to help, The PROCARMA Team".

**Next step in each email**

| Emails | Main button | Smaller link | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | "Schedule a demo" | "Start your subscription" | This is the brand's main ask, and managers will likely want to see it first. |
| 5–6 | "Start your subscription" | "Schedule a demo" | There's less time for demos late in the campaign, so we ask for the decision. |

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **15% is a high target for email alone.** | Check the running total every Monday. If it's under 8 on Dec 14, change the subject lines, wording or sign-up page before Email 3, and send personal follow-ups to stores that clicked. |
| **December may be a busy time for stores, and the holidays lower attention.** | Do most of the work in the first two weeks, and send nothing Dec 23–28. |
| **Decisions may take longer than four weeks, especially if a general manager must sign off.** | Make Email 4 easy to forward. Keep talking to interested stores after January 3. |
| **Too many people from one store may get the same emails.** | Match people to stores, and stop emailing a store once it subscribes. |
| **The Maintenance wording may break a brand rule.** | PROCARMA approves the service summary by Nov 13 and the final wording by Dec 4. |
| **Pages or demos may not be ready.** | Don't send Email 1 until both pages are tested and demo times are set. Move the launch if needed. |
| **Unsubscribes or complaints may rise.** | Check after every send and pause if there's a sudden jump. Every email carries an unsubscribe link and our postal address, and your team should check the US email rules with whoever handles legal matters. |
