# Pit Stop Maintenance: launch plan for dealerships

**Status:** Draft. The offer, prices, launch wording and demo plan are approved. The steps in part 10 come before any email goes out.

**Who this is for:** F&I managers, service managers and general managers at about 200 dealerships on our waitlist.

**Product name:** "Pit Stop Maintenance" is the name used in the approved launch wording for our new Maintenance service. It has not been checked for trademark use.

**Sending:** This studio only plans and drafts. Your team sends the real emails from your own email tool, after approval.

## The offer

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Who we email | About 200 dealerships on the waitlist. Success means 30 of them subscribe. |
| What a dealership gets | A new service its F&I managers can offer to car buyers as an option, at a price the store sets. Also a setup call, team training, tracking and a monthly summary. PROCARMA runs the systems behind the service. |
| Launch offer for its customers | Each customer who buys gets one complimentary oil change after 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first, and 5% off one tire rotation after 6 months, used in the store's own service lane. Both are counted from the purchase and must be used within 1 year of it. This is treated as a launch offer, separate from what the contract covers. |
| Who pays for the launch offer | The dealership pays for both. |
| Regular price | $299 per month per store. |
| Launch price | $199 per month for the first 12 months, for stores that sign up by January 3, 2027. |
| Cancelling | Month to month, with 30 days' notice. |
| Demos | One video demo per dealership on the waitlist, run by the CES admin team, with times from Dec 8 and after Dec 22. |

**Setup steps**

1. PROCARMA holds a short setup call with the general manager.
2. PROCARMA trains the F&I and service teams.
3. F&I managers offer the service to car buyers as an option at the sale.
4. The service desk applies the launch offer when the customer comes in.
5. PROCARMA does the tracking, runs the systems behind the service and sends a monthly summary of plans sold and customers who came back for service.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Pit Stop Maintenance launch
- **Summary:** Six emails over four weeks invite about 200 waitlisted dealerships to book a demo and start a subscription, with most of the effort in the first two weeks.
- **Main goal:** 30 of about 200 dealerships (15%) start a subscription between December 7, 2026 and January 3, 2027.
- **Second goals:** Book demos with stores that aren't ready yet, and learn which group responds best.

**Name options**

| Name | Why it could work |

| Service Lane Maintenance | It's the plainest option, and it speaks directly to service teams. |

## 2. Target audience

We group people by job role. We don't yet know how many people are in each group. The group names are for planning only and won't appear in emails.

| Group | Who they are | Main interest | What they may worry about | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Deal Desk** | F&I managers | More revenue | Does this slow down the sale? How do I explain it clearly? | Interested, needs details |
| **The Service Lane** | Service managers | Happier customers who come back | Can my team handle the visits? How does it work at the desk? | Interested, needs details |
| **The Store Leaders** | General managers | Results for the whole store, with less admin | What does it cost? Who runs it? Is it worth the team's time? | Likely makes the final decision |

- **Matching job roles:** Job titles will vary, so sort them into these three groups before launch. Anyone who doesn't fit joins The Store Leaders.
- **What they were told:** A new product was launching to help their customers come to the dealership. The emails say "can help", not "will help" (rule 1).
- **Where they are:** They're busy on the dealership floor and read email in short bursts, so emails must be short and easy to read on a phone.

**Proof:** We have no approved results yet, so we won't use numbers, results or dealer names. Emails explain how the service works instead.

**Brand rules to check on every email**

- Say "can help" or "designed to", never "will" (rule 1).
- Don't use "guaranteed" or "risk-free" (rule 2).
- Don't describe what a contract covers. Mention only the approved launch offer, and point to the contract for the rest (rule 3).
- Never suggest a car buyer must buy this to get a car, a loan or a better rate (rule 4).
- Say "complimentary", never "free", and say who pays (rule 5).
- Don't name car makers, administrators, dealers or competitors without written permission (rule 6).
- Use "no gateway fees" or "top tier administrators and agencies nationwide" only if you've checked they're still true (rule 12).
- Keep the 30 target and all other goals in this plan out of the emails (rule 1).
- Sign off with "Here to help, The PROCARMA Team".

## 8. Budget

- **Ads:** None.
- **Email:** Your existing email tool. Check that your plan covers the full list and six sends.
- **Writing and demos:** Mostly your team's time. Video demos will likely take the most time.
- **Launch offer:** Each dealership pays for its own customers' oil changes and tire rotation discounts, so this isn't a campaign cost.
- **After launch:** Setup calls, training and monthly summaries take your team's time, so include them when you check the prices.
- **Spare money:** If you set a budget figure, keep 10–15% aside for surprises, such as sign-up form fixes.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **15% is a high target for email alone.** | Check the running total every Monday. If it's under 8 on Dec 14, change the subject lines, wording or sign-up form before Email 3, and send personal follow-ups to stores that clicked. |
| **December may be a busy time for stores, and the holidays lower attention.** | Do most of the work in the first two weeks, and send nothing Dec 23–28. |
| **Stores may push back on paying for the launch offer.** | Bring it up early in the demo, and explain that the work happens in their own service lane and can help bring customers back. |
| **We may run short of video demo times before January 3.** | Up to about 200 stores could each ask for one demo, and Dec 8–22 has 11 working days. Set how many the CES admin team can do each day, and add times if requests build up. |
| **Decisions may take longer than four weeks, especially if a general manager must sign off.** | Make Email 4 easy to forward. Keep talking to interested stores after January 3. |
| **Too many people from one store may get the same emails.** | Match people to stores, and stop emailing a store once it subscribes. Combine requests from one store into one demo. |
| **The Maintenance wording may break a brand rule.** | PROCARMA approves the service summary by Nov 13 and the final wording by Dec 4. |
| **Forms or demos may not be ready.** | Don't send Email 1 until both forms are tested and demo times are set. Move the launch if needed. |
| **Unsubscribes or complaints may rise.** | Check after every send and pause if there's a sudden jump. Every email carries an unsubscribe link and our postal address, and your team should check the US email rules with whoever handles legal matters. |
