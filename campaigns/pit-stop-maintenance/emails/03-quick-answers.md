Here are quick answers about Pit Stop Maintenance. Forward this to anyone who helps decide at your store.

**What does it cost?**

$199 per month for your first 12 months when you sign up by January 3, 2027. Then $299 per month. Those prices are per store.

**Can we cancel?**

Yes. You pay month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

**What does our store get?**

A new service your F&I managers can offer car buyers as an option, at a price your store sets. You also get a setup call, team training, tracking and a monthly summary.

**Do car buyers have to buy it?**

No. It's an option. Car buyers don't need it to get a car, a loan or a better rate.

**What's the launch offer, and who pays for it?**

Your store pays. Each customer who buys gets one complimentary oil change after 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first, and 5% off one tire rotation after 6 months. Both are used in your service lane, are counted from the purchase and must be used within 1 year of it. See the contract for full terms.

**How does setup work?**

1. We hold a short setup call with your general manager.
2. We train your F&I and service teams.
3. Your F&I managers offer it to car buyers as an option at the sale.
4. Your service desk applies the launch offer when the customer comes in.
5. We do the tracking, run the systems and send a monthly summary of plans sold and customers who came back for service.

That split is designed to keep admin low for your team.
