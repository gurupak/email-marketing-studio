Here are quick answers about Pit Stop Maintenance. Forward this to anyone who helps decide at your store.

**What does it cost?**

$199 per month per store for your first 12 months if you sign up by January 3, 2027. After that, it's $299 per month. Stores that sign up later pay $299 from the start.

**Can we cancel?**

Yes. You pay month to month and can cancel with 30 days' notice.

**What does our store get?**

A new Maintenance service your F&I managers can offer car buyers as an option, at a price your store sets. You also get a setup call and team training. We track sales and return visits and send you a monthly summary.

**Do car buyers have to buy it?**

No. It's an option. Car buyers don't need it to get a car, a loan or a better rate.

**What's the launch offer, and who pays for it?**

Customers who buy it get one complimentary oil change after 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first, and 5% off one tire rotation after 6 months. Both are used in your service lane, are counted from the purchase and must be used within 1 year of it. Your store covers the cost of both. This launch offer is separate from the contract. See the contract for what it covers.

**How does setup work?**

1. We hold a short setup call with your general manager.
2. We train your F&I and service teams.
3. Your F&I managers offer it to car buyers as an option at the sale.
4. Your service desk applies the launch offer when the customer comes in.
5. We track sales and return visits, run the systems behind the service and send you a monthly summary.

That split is designed to keep admin low for your team.
