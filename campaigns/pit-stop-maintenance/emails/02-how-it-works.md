Pit Stop Maintenance is designed to keep admin low. Here's who does what, from the sale to the service visit.

**At the sale: your F&I managers**

They offer it to car buyers as an option, at a price your store sets. It's one more clear option that can help grow revenue.

Tip: Start with the word "optional." Try this: "This one's optional. You don't need it to get your car, your loan or your rate. The contract has the full terms."

**At the visit: your service team**

The service desk applies the launch offer when the customer comes in. That can help bring customers back to your service lane.

Tip: Check the purchase date first. Both parts of the offer are counted from it.

**Launch offer:** Customers who buy get one complimentary oil change after 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first, and 5% off one tire rotation after 6 months. Both are used in your service lane and must be used within 1 year of the purchase. Your store covers the cost of both. See the contract for full terms.

**Behind the scenes: PROCARMA**

We hold a short setup call with your general manager and train your F&I and service teams. Then we do the tracking, run the systems behind the service and send you a monthly summary of plans sold and customers who came back for service.

Tip for general managers: Each store gets one video demo, so bring your F&I and service managers along.
